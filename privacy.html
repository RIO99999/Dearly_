Privacy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Dearly is built to create a shareable card without an account or Dearly server. This page explains what happens in the static site.

Card content

Your names, message, theme, occasion, and photo URL are processed in your browser and encoded into the share link. Dearly does not upload or store that card data. The recipient can read it from the link, so treat the link as private to the extent you want the card to be private.

Photos and external services

If you add a photo URL, the viewer's browser requests that image directly from the host named in the URL. That host may receive standard request information such as an IP address and browser details under its own privacy policy. Dearly does not proxy the photo or operate the photo host.

Local browser features

Dearly may use the browser's clipboard feature when you choose Copy link. The site does not require an account and does not intentionally use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or a backend API for card creation.

Hosting and links

The site is delivered by the static hosting provider chosen by its operator. That provider may process technical request logs to serve and secure the site. Third-party font files may be requested from Google Fonts when a network connection is available; system fonts remain available if they cannot load.

Children and sensitive information

Dearly is intended for ordinary personal greetings, not for collecting sensitive personal information. Do not include secrets, financial details, health details, or information you would not want copied by someone with the share link.
