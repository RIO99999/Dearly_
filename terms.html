How Dearly works

Card details are encoded into the share link in your browser. Dearly does not send card text to a Dearly server or keep a card database. Anyone who has the complete share link can view the card, so only share it with people you trust.

Photo links are loaded directly from the address you provide and may be visible to people who receive the card link.
